Why does .card need position: relative for the badge to land in its corner?
Because when the page is resized or on different devices, then the badge would need to be relative to the position of the card
in order to resize properly.

What does z-index control, and when do you need it?
Z-index controls the visual stacking order of the elements you add and you need it when you have multiple elements overlapping with one another.

What does "mobile-first" mean, and how do the two media queries build on the base styles?
Mobile first would mean when you design your website for the smaller screens as a base line then increase the screen size and adjust for that.

What does object-fit: cover do to an image, and why pair it with max-width: 100%?
It make the image fit the entire width of the screen by scaling it and you would pair it with max width in order to make sure the
image never gets bigger than the screen width.

What goes in the <head> of a page versus the <body>, and who sees each?
The head of the page tells the user what the page is so typically a title and the body tells the user what the page is about 
usually information about what the programmer decides.

What is the difference between running your page at localhost:3000 and at <your-IP>:8080?
Localhost is when you would want only your device to have access to the website while having your ip would mean other devices 
can access your program.

In your own words, what does push do and what does pull do in today's loop?
Push moves your code to github while pull "pulls" the code for it to be launched in the droplet.

Why do we test on localhost:3000 before pushing to production?
To make sure that it has no errors before allowing it to be accessed by others.
